Getting Here

Transport Options

By Air
John F. Kennedy International Airport is 22 miles away (approx. 35 minutes by car). Our hotel offers premium airport transfer service, bookable at reception or through your reservation.
By Train
Grand Central Terminal is a 5-minute walk from the hotel. Trains arrive from all major cities. The Metro-North Railroad provides connections to suburban areas.
By Subway
The closest subway station is "Azure Plaza" on the Blue Line, just a 2-minute walk. Multiple bus routes also stop directly outside the hotel.
By Car
Self-parking and valet parking are available at our underground garage. Enter from Azure Boulevard. Height restriction: 2.1m. Complimentary for all guests.
By Taxi / Uber
Taxis and rideshare vehicles can drop off and pick up at our main entrance on Azure Boulevard. Our concierge can arrange transportation 24/7.
